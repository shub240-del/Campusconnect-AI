SmallBiz Online Shop — Returns and Refunds Policy

Effective Date: 1 October 2026

1. RETURNS WINDOW
   You may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund or exchange.
   The 30-day window begins on the date shown in your delivery confirmation email.

2. ITEMS ELIGIBLE FOR RETURN
   Most items are eligible for return provided they are:
   - Unused or in original condition (e.g. not worn, assembled, or damaged by the customer)
   - In their original packaging with all accessories and documentation
   - Accompanied by proof of purchase (order ID or receipt)

3. NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
   The following items cannot be returned:
   - Digital downloads or software once activated
   - Personalised or custom-made items
   - Perishable goods
   - Items marked as "Final Sale" at the time of purchase
   - Hygiene-sensitive products (e.g. earphones) once opened, unless faulty

4. DAMAGED OR FAULTY ITEMS
   If your item arrives damaged or develops a fault within the warranty period:
   - Contact us within 14 days of delivery for damaged-on-arrival items
   - We will arrange a free return collection at no cost to you
   - We will send a replacement item or issue a full refund, including original delivery costs
   - You do not need to return a faulty item if we confirm it is unsafe to do so

5. HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN
   Step 1: Contact our support team via WhatsApp/SMS or email with your order ID and reason for return.
   Step 2: We will send you a prepaid return label within 1 business day.
   Step 3: Pack the item securely and drop it off at any Royal Mail Post Office.
   Step 4: Once we receive and inspect the item (within 3 business days), we will process your refund.

6. REFUND TIMELINE
   - Credit/debit card refunds: 3–5 business days after we receive the returned item
   - PayPal refunds: 1–2 business days after we receive the returned item
   - Original delivery costs are refunded only if the return is due to our error or a faulty item
   - Return delivery costs are covered by SmallBiz when we provide the prepaid label

7. EXCHANGES
   We do not process direct exchanges. To exchange an item, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the replacement.

8. CONTACT
   For all returns and refund enquiries, contact our support team via the messaging channel you used to order, or email support@smallbiz.example.com.

This policy does not affect your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (UK).
