SmallBiz Online Shop — Shipping and Delivery Policy

Effective Date: 1 October 2026

1. SHIPPING ZONES
   We currently ship to all addresses within the United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland).
   We do not currently ship to the Channel Islands, Isle of Man, BFPO addresses, or internationally.

2. DELIVERY OPTIONS AND COSTS
   Standard Delivery (3–5 business days): £2.99 per order. FREE on orders over £40.
   Express Delivery (next business day): £5.99 per order. Order must be placed before 2:00pm GMT/BST.
   Saturday Delivery: £7.99 per order. Order must be placed before 2:00pm on Friday.
   Click & Collect (London warehouse): FREE. Available by appointment only. Contact us to arrange.

3. ORDER PROCESSING TIMES
   Orders placed before 2:00pm Monday–Friday are processed the same day.
   Orders placed after 2:00pm, on weekends, or on UK public holidays are processed the next working day.
   During peak periods (e.g. Black Friday, Christmas) processing may take an additional 1–2 days.

4. TRACKING YOUR ORDER
   Once your order is dispatched you will receive a dispatch confirmation email containing:
   - Your order ID (e.g. ORD-7781)
   - Your carrier tracking number
   - A link to track your delivery in real time
   You can also message us directly with your order ID to get a status update via our AI support agent.

5. DELIVERY PARTNERS
   We work with Royal Mail for Standard and Saturday deliveries and DPD for Express deliveries.
   Delivery partners may vary by region and during high-demand periods.

6. FAILED DELIVERY ATTEMPTS
   If a delivery attempt is unsuccessful, the carrier will:
   - Leave a "Sorry we missed you" card with instructions
   - Make up to 2 additional delivery attempts
   - Hold the parcel at a local depot or Post Office for 18 days before returning it to us
   If a parcel is returned to us, we will contact you and reship at no extra charge (one reshipment per order).

7. DELIVERY TO SAFE PLACE OR NEIGHBOUR
   You may request in the order notes that the carrier leave your parcel in a safe place or with a neighbour.
   SmallBiz is not liable for loss or damage to parcels left as instructed.

8. IMPORT DUTIES AND TAXES
   All prices shown on our website include UK VAT at the applicable rate. No additional import duties apply to UK domestic orders.

9. LOST OR DELAYED PARCELS
   If your parcel has not arrived within 10 business days of dispatch (Standard) or 3 business days (Express), please contact our support team.
   We will open an investigation with the carrier and, if the parcel is confirmed lost, send a replacement or issue a full refund within 5 business days of confirmation.

10. CONTACT
    For all shipping and delivery enquiries, message our support team via WhatsApp/SMS, or email support@smallbiz.example.com.
